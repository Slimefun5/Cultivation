Cultivation adds plants, bushes and trees grown on crop sticks, and a kitchen to cook what they yield. Plants breed
with their neighbours into new kinds or stronger versions of themselves, the Garden Cloche grows them indoors, and
Wandering Traders sell notes on breeding pairs. The plants are display entities, so lag-clearing plugins must leave
`BLOCK_DISPLAY`, `ITEM_DISPLAY`, `TEXT_DISPLAY` and `INTERACTION` entities alone.

## :floppy_disk: Installation

| Cultivation | Slimefun5 | Minecraft |
|---|---|---|
| UNOFFICIAL | 5.3.3+ | 1.19.4 to 26.x |

Download the latest release and place it in your server's `plugins/` folder. Slimefun5 must be installed first. On a
server older than 1.19.4 Cultivation logs one line and disables itself.
